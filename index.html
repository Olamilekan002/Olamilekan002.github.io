Biography

I am an AI researcher and engineer who builds and evaluates large language models and the agents built on them. At Turing I lead teams that find where frontier models fail, and I build simulated environments, stateful mock APIs, in which a model learns to plan and carry out multi-step tool actions. At Codematic I put LLM and agent workflows into production.

My research began in medical imaging, with fair and efficient diagnosis models presented at the WiML Workshop at NeurIPS 2024. It has since moved to language technology for African languages: I am collaborating on a benchmark that tests whether models can call functions correctly from requests in Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo.

Next, I want to study how autonomous agents reason about the knowledge, beliefs and intentions of other agents, so that mixed teams of symbolic and learned agents can coordinate reliably under uncertainty.

I am applying for PhD programmes starting in 2027 in epistemic planning, multi-agent systems and the reliability of LLM agents. If our interests overlap, I would be glad to hear from you.
